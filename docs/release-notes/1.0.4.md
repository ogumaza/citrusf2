A release that comes closer to the game's tone. The 3DS's DSP darkens a note that plays more than one sample of its wave per output sample. Such notes now play darker in the SoundFonts and SFZ files too. Convert an archive again to get the corrected files.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bcsar` files onto `citrusf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `citrusf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each archive.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. citrusf2 builds on three libraries in Git submodules. Clone the repository with `--recursive`. macOS builds include `Citrusf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **A new report line.** The SoundFont leaves the new resampling low-pass out of a preset whose filter changes during notes. citrusf2 reports the notes above a rate of 1 that such a preset plays.
- **FluidSynth's level.** The README explains a difference in FluidSynth's level. FluidSynth plays every note 1.5 dB louder than the SoundFont specifies. Multiplying FluidSynth's gain by 0.84 gives the specified level.

## Fixes

- **The DSP's resampling.** The DSP resamples each voice through one of three filters. The voice's rate decides which. The rate is the voice's pitch times its wave's sample rate, over the output's 32,728 Hz. Above a rate of 1, the filter cuts more of the highs than the synths' interpolation. Above 4/3, it cuts more still. SoundFont zones and SFZ regions above a rate of 1 now have a low-pass whose cutoff tracks the key. The cutoffs are fitted to the DSP's filters. Such notes used to play brighter than in the game.

## Known limitations

As in 1.0.3, apart from the fixes above. A zone's resampling low-pass follows the rate at the zone's key. In the game, the filter changes whenever a bend, sweep or LFO moves the rate across 1 or 4/3. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
