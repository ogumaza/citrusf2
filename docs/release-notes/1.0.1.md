A bug-fix release: sounds that came out too long, too quiet or out of tune now follow the game more closely. Convert an archive again to get the corrected files.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `citrusf2-1.0.1-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md` and `LICENSE` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.bcsar` files onto `citrusf2.exe`, or use a terminal; `citrusf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each archive.

For macOS and Linux, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Citrusf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Fixes

- **Held loops.** A main loop that only holds tied notes now ends 10 seconds after its last change, like other held sounds, instead of running for minutes. A sequence that reaches the 15-minute limit after more than 10 seconds of silence ends with its last sound.
- **Release tails.** When a track ends, its notes' release tails get the volume, pan, pitch and filter of the track's last tick, as in the game, rather than those of the tick before.
- **Slow decays.** The game's slowest decays take up to six times as long as SoundFont allows. Such a decay now gets a longer hold, so the note reaches its sustain level when the game's does instead of falling too quiet too soon.
- **Citrusf2.app** says when an archive is truncated, rather than showing it as converted in full.
